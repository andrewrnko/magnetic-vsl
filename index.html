QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Do you only work with local businesses?

No. We are based in Seattle and a lot of our production work is local because shoot days are physical. Strategy, acquisition and systems work is not tied to a city.

Do you only make content?

No. Content is one output. Most of the value is in positioning, acquisition and the systems that catch what the content produces.

Do you run paid ads?

Yes. Apex runs paid media, funnels and retargeting, and we make the creative that goes into them, which is usually the part that decides the result.

Do we need existing content?

No. If there is usable footage we will use it. If there isn't, the first shoot fixes that.

How much content do you produce?

Enough to keep a thirty day buffer ahead of the schedule. The exact volume depends on the channels and the objective, not on a package tier.

How long before launch?

Diagnosis and positioning come first, then the first shoot, then deployment. It is measured in weeks, and we tell you the dates before we start.

Do you work with competitors?

Not in the same market. We take one business per category per area so the positioning work stays worth something.

Can you work with our internal team?

Yes, and it usually works better that way. Your team keeps the context; our system takes the repetitive load.

How involved do I need to be?

Heavily in the first two weeks, lightly after that. Approvals take minutes on your phone.

What does pricing look like?

It depends on the scope of what has to be built. We quote after the diagnosis, not before, because a number given before we understand the bottleneck is a guess.

Do you do one-off projects?

Rarely. Systems need time to show whether they work. Occasionally a single, well-defined build makes sense, and we will say so.

What happens after we apply?

We review it, and if it looks like a fit we book a call to diagnose. If it isn't, we tell you why rather than leaving it unanswered.
